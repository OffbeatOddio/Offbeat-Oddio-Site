Links about music, design, and distribution:

  • feuilleton album covers archive, which includes some of our favorites such as Dick Ellescas, Leo and Diane Dillon, Philippe Caza, Philippe Druillet, and, of course, Bob Pepper.
  • typo.polona.pl/en, digital collage letter tool developed by the National Library of Poland.

Links for fun:

  • isopod.site, for the isopod curious.
  • donjon.bin.sh, RPG tools and resources, a great worldbuilding reference.
  • vulgarlang, a fantasy language generator.
  • The Academy of St. Gabriel, a volunteer-organized reference site for medieval names and heraldry for use by the SCA and other re-enactment groups.
  • myunicofans.com, a charming fansite for a great Sanrio movie.
  • strandbeest, beests that live on the strand?

Reading Recommendations:

  • How to Do Nothing: Resisting the Attention Economy by Jenny Odell - A good read for those wishing to be an active participant in life (and society) rather than a consumer.
  • The Dispossessed by Ursula K. LeGuin - A man from the moon returns to earth. A good read for those interested in anarchism.
